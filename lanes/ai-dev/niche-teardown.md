This is the AI/dev equivalent of the history-channel teardown — same structure: lane comps, RPM bands, asset feasibility against our stack, strategic call. AI/dev is one of the **highest-RPM lanes we've mapped** ($15+ on the B2B/SaaS end) with **near-zero strike risk** and a production profile that fits our existing Remotion + ElevenLabs pipeline almost perfectly.

## TL;DR — The Call

**Lane:** Faceless **evergreen project-tutorials as the spine (~60%)** + **trending tool-news/Shorts as the discovery layer (~40%)**.

**Why this beats AI-news aggregation:** the news lane (Wes Roth / Matthew Berman style) is saturated, decays in *days*, and sits squarely in YouTube's July-2025 "inauthentic/reused content" demonetization crosshairs. The tutorial/build lane monetizes *faster*, pays a *higher* RPM, compounds via search, and survives the AI-slop crackdown because real screen-recorded dev work can't be faked.

**Production fit:** Our stack covers ~80% out of the box. Three additions unlock the rest: a **screen recorder** (Screen Studio / Cap.so), **Manim + ManimML** for research-grade concept animation, and **de-risking b-roll off Higgsfield** onto Veo 3.1 + Kling 3.0.

**Moat:** editorial rigor or animation craft (the Two Minute Papers / AI Explained / 3Blue1Brown playbook) + genuine screen-recorded dev work. Volume alone loses.

## 5. Strike / Copyright Risk — Near-Zero, One Real Caveat

- **Screen recordings of software/terminals/our own code** → effectively zero exposure; original work product.
- **AI-generated b-roll / our own renders** → no third-party copyright. (Per 2025 US Copyright Office guidance, pure AI output isn't *protectable* by us, but it doesn't infringe anyone either.)
- **Paper figures / product screenshots / short keynote clips** → commentary/criticism is strong fair-use; worst realistic case is a Content ID *claim* (demonetizes one video, not a strike), usually from background music. Avoid licensed music + long verbatim keynote footage → risk drops to noise.
- **THE REAL RISK is not copyright — it's YouTube's "inauthentic/reused content" policy (updated July 15, 2025).** It explicitly targets low-effort AI commentary, reaction clips, and compilations with no transformation. A faceless AI-news channel narrating headlines over stock B-roll is the *exact profile* it demonetizes. **Mitigation = genuine editorial value: original analysis, our own tests, custom visuals, distinct scripted voice.** Transformation is the whole game — and it's fully controllable through craft.

## 6. SEO & Topic Strategy (0-sub launch)

**Structural edge:** AI/dev moves so fast that search demand outruns supply on a 2–6 week lag. When a tool ships, thousands search "how do I use X" before any large channel covers it well.

**Topic veins (high-search, low-competition for a small channel):**

| Vein | Example 2026 topics | Competition |
|---|---|---|
| **Emerging AI-dev tool tutorials** | "MCP server tutorial", "Claude Code subagents", "Cursor rules setup", "Claude Code hooks" | Low → fills fast |
| **Agentic workflow builds** | "build a WhatsApp AI agent n8n", "multi-agent system tutorial", "RAG pipeline 2026" | Low–Med |
| **"X vs Y" tool comparisons** | "Cursor vs Claude Code vs Windsurf", "Sonnet 4.6 vs GPT vs Gemini coding" | Med (high CTR) |
| **Specific LLM API how-tos** | "Anthropic API tool use", "structured outputs with X", "prompt caching tutorial" | Low |
| **Niche dev-concept explainers** | "MCP explained", "vector embeddings in 100 seconds", "what is an AI agent" | Low–Med |
| **"Build X with [AI tool]"** | "build a SaaS with Claude Code", "ship a product with AI agents" | Med (highest intent) |

*Note: the MCP ecosystem went from a few dozen servers (early 2025) to 500+ by April 2026 — that catalog is a content firehose: one explainer per notable server.*

**SEO hygiene:** target long-tail (specific intent, thin supply) not head terms; mine YouTube autocomplete for literal queries; tutorials hit best at **8–15 min** (also wins watch-time, the dominant ranking signal).

| Format | Cadence | Lane | Why it scales |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"[Tool] in 100 seconds"** | 2–3/wk | Explainer/evergreen | Fixed skeleton + screen-record + meme cutaways; Fireship-validated |

| **"X vs Y" comparison** | 1/wk | Evergreen + trending | Reusable table/benchmark template; ranks on buyer searches |
| **Weekly AI roundup** | 1/wk fixed slot | Trending/discovery | Predictable cadence, builds return-viewer habit |
| **"X explained"** deep-dive | 1–2/wk | Evergreen | Pure long-tail SEO; compounds; no time decay |
| **Paper / repo of the week** | 1/wk | Research-explainer | Endless supply (HF papers, trending GitHub); under-served faceless lane |

**Title/thumbnail patterns that win:** "X is dead" (controversy), "I tried X so you don't have to" (curiosity + time-save), version/time hooks ("I built a SaaS in 1 hour with AI"), "[Tool] in 100 seconds", "X vs Y vs Z". Thumbnails: **blue dominates** for tech (trust signal), **3–5 words max**, high contrast, custom thumbs get +35% CTR. Stacked title formulas beat single by 15–25%. **A/B test every upload** (YouTube shipped native title+thumbnail testing globally Dec 2025; lifts CTR 10–25%).

## 8. Evergreen vs Trending — The Math

| | Trending (news) | Evergreen (tutorial) |
|---|---|---|
| View curve | ~80% of views in first 7–14 days | >50% of views land *after* day 30 |
| Half-life | Days | Months–years |
| ROI | Fast, non-compounding | **~4× vs trend content** (sustained search) |
| Sub retention | Lower | 60%+ evergreen → 2.3× better 12-mo retention |

**Optimal mix: ~60% evergreen / 40% trending.** It's a funnel, not a coin-flip: trending videos are the *discovery layer* when a hot tool drops; evergreen tutorials are the *conversion + monetization layer* that pays out for years. Format mapping: **Shorts (cut from long-form) = discovery + sub-velocity; long-form = monetization.** Shorts views don't count toward the 4000-hour YPP bar and earn ~95% lower CPM — use them to funnel, not to monetize.

## 9. Path to Monetization (YPP: 1000 subs + 4000 watch-hours/12mo)

**Realistic timeline for a well-run faceless tech channel posting 3–5×/week:** months 1–3 library-building, months 4–6 at 500–800 subs, **most cross YPP months 6–12** with first payouts $50–500/mo. Tailwind: faceless formats saw 18× YoY growth in AI/tech; tech *tutorials* command $8–12 RPM (vs $2–5 generic).

**Fastest formula:** evergreen **"Build X in Y min" + "[Tool] explained" long-form** banks the compounding watch-hours at the niche's highest RPM; **Shorts cut from those same videos** drive subscriber velocity. The build/tutorial lane beats AI-news to YPP because: higher RPM, compounding (not decaying) watch-hours, and it survives the AI-slop demonetization crackdown by being genuinely useful.

## Bottom-Line Launch Recipe

1. **Lane:** evergreen project tutorials as the spine (60%); trending tool-news + Shorts as discovery (40%).
2. **Templates:** "[Tool] in 100s", "Build X in Y min", "X vs Y", weekly roundup — re-skinned per topic for cadence.
3. **Topics:** chase emerging-tool long-tail (MCP, Claude Code, Cursor, agentic/n8n, new frameworks) where keywords are still empty; beat the big channels on speed.
4. **Titles:** stacked formulas, blue 3–5-word thumbs, A/B test every upload.
5. **Monetize:** long-form banks 4000 hours at $8–12 RPM; Shorts bank 1000 subs. Target YPP months 6–12.
6. **Moat:** real screen-recorded dev work + editorial rigor, not AI slop. That's what survives demonetization and earns the niche's RPM.
